Mini-Chat orb — expression & movement prototype

A throwaway demo to feel out how the floating orb behaves: its expression states, where its eyes look, how it glides & bobs, how dragging works, and how it clamps to the screen. Drive it with the buttons or the walkthroughs below, then tell me what feels wrong.

Questions this settles: (1) Is the 4-state chat lifecycle idle → thinking → speaking → done with gaze as a separate axis (eyes track a target during any state) better than making "looking" a 5th sequential state? (2) Does a move action temporarily override the user's dragged spot, then return home — or stay put? (3) Is a ~4px drag threshold enough to tell a drag from a click-to-open?
