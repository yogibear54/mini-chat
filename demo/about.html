Our story

Lotus Creations started in 2024 as a weekend project: a floating assistant that actually knew the page it lived on. It grew into a small studio building calm, useful little things for the web.

We believe widgets should be polite: visible when wanted, out of the way otherwise, and never, ever auto-playing.

Values

  • Calm software. Motion with a purpose, or none at all.
  • One script tag. If it takes a build pipeline to host us, we've failed.
  • Honest assistants. It says “I don't know” instead of inventing.
